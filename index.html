    Trusted members

    Saved on this device only

    Add a member by uploading their UPI QR image. The image is decoded in this browser; only the member name, mobile number, UPI ID, and QR fingerprint are saved locally. A trusted match can permit app handoff even if this QR has local scam reports, but the scam warning remains visible and you must confirm before continuing.

    Check a link before opening

    Checked locally · not submitted

    Paste a URL to inspect its address and local scam reports. This does not visit the site or confirm its identity. Clean results are only “no obvious issue found,” never a verified guarantee.

    Open in new tab — unverified
    Important: QR and link reports, plus trusted-member details, are saved only in this browser; they are not sent to banks or other users. Link checks use local URL patterns and reports—not a live reputation service. “No obvious issue found” is not verification. Opening a link is always a separate user action, and flagged links are blocked by this page. QR checks cannot prove identity or guarantee payment safety. Never share your UPI PIN to receive money.
